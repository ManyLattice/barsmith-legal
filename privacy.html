Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Introduction

Mikhail Gudzikevich ("we", "our", "us") operates Barsmith: Plate Calculator, an app that shows which plates to load on a barbell for a target weight. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and where it is stored.

In short: everything you enter stays on your device. The app does not connect to our servers, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not collect any data about you.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Your gyms: names, barbell weights, units (kg or lb) and the plates available in each gym, including how many pairs of each.
  • Your settings, such as the default unit, weight steps, plate colors and whether plate labels are shown.
  • Whether you have bought Barsmith Lifetime.

This information is stored only on your device using Apple's on-device storage. We do not upload it and the app does not sync it to any cloud service. If device backups are enabled in your iOS settings, it may be included in your iCloud or computer backup, which is controlled by you and Apple. You can edit or delete your gyms at any time, and deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Live Activities

With Barsmith Lifetime, the app shows the plates to load on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity. You can turn Live Activities off in iOS Settings. It is created and updated on your device and is not sent to us.

Purchases (Apple)

  • Barsmith Lifetime is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.
  • The app checks with Apple on your device whether you own the purchase, so it can unlock Lifetime features. "Restore purchase" asks Apple to sync your purchases.
  • This processing is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require accounts, logins or email addresses.
  • We do not operate servers, and the app does not send your gyms, weights or settings anywhere.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting services, and the app contains no third-party SDKs.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • We do not have access to your payment information.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not collect personal information, so we do not sell or share it. The only third party involved is Apple, which processes purchases as described above.

Data Retention

  • Your gyms and settings stay on your device until you delete them or delete the app.
  • We do not keep copies of your data.

Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's security, including its passcode and data protection. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
