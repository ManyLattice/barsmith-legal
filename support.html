Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. The app has no accounts and does not send your gyms, weights or settings anywhere. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Why does the app show a different weight than I entered?

If the exact weight cannot be made from the plates you have, Barsmith shows the nearest weight it can load. Check that your barbell weight and available plates are set correctly for your gym.

Can I use kilograms and pounds?

Yes. Each gym has its own unit and its own set of plates, and you can choose the default unit in Settings.

What does Barsmith Lifetime include?

Gym Inventory (custom plate sets for each gym), Live Activities with the plates you need on your Lock Screen, and custom plate colors. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I bought Lifetime on another iPhone. How do I get it back?

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchase", signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
